# Research findings (Opus 5.5, 2026-11-11): game as a knowledge + information hub
Raw findings for the spec. Every item with source URL.

## A. Jewish / Torah games and apps that already combine play + content
1. Torah Live (torahlive.com) – Torah videos for kids + game layer: points for videos/quizzes/games, badges, 36 levels, per-course games, "challenges" to create something; points also count for a TEAM (family, school, shul, organisation) on a leaderboard; "Classic Mode" hides the game layer for families who don't want it. Has a dedicated Chabad page. Sources: https://help.torahlive.com/en/articles/6251321-torah-live-the-world-of-gaming ; https://torahlive.com/chabad
2. JewQ International Torah Championship (Chabad, CKids / Merkos 302) – annual contest since 2018, grades 3–7; handbooks per grade, three written tests, regional quiz bowls, international final. ~4,000 kids, 250 communities (2024); ~4,000 from 26 countries (2025). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JewQ_International_Torah_Championship
3. Tzivos Hashem (Chabad, since 1980) – children's "army": missions (tzedaka, prayer, kindness) earn points, medals, ranks Private → Five Star General; monthly rallies with prizes; magazines (Moshiach Times etc.). Historical campaigns reached 50,000 kids. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tzivos_Hashem
4. TorahVR "MikDash" (!!) – Chabad-founded startup. VR game in the Beit HaMikdash: explore rooms, mikveh, kelim, collect tokens/coins, learn to bring offerings, oils unlock areas, guide character "Reishi". 2022 launch in camps (1,000+ campers, 30,000 minutes first month, ~15-min sessions, kosher headsets, schools buying 25-headset sets). Meta Quest store release 30 June 2025, $19.99, early access. NOTE: SAME NAME "MikDash" as our repo/game – name clash risk for a commercial release. Sources: https://chabadinfo.com/news/torahvr-releases-worlds-first-jewish-vr-game-over-30000-minutes-of-gameplay-clocked-in-first-month ; https://queststoredb.com/game/mikdash-9844812318933136/
5. Jewish Interactive / Jigzi – kids' Jewish learning games platform. Source: https://www.jewishinteractive.org/jigzi-home/
6. Other Temple apps: "Jerusalem Temple" App Store app; Temple Institute 3D walkthrough videos. Sources: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6740471825 ; https://jewishpress.com/multimedia/video-picks/the-third-temple-a-3d-walk-through/2015/07/26

## B. Religious-public information hubs in Israel (leaflets, events, community)
7. Shabbat leaflets (עלוני שבת): e.g. "שיחת השבוע" by צעירי אגודת חב"ד, ~40 years, 2000+ issues, tens of thousands of copies weekly in hundreds of synagogues (Maariv). Criticism in Kipa column "מעלעל": repetitive topics, hidden promotion, inconsistent halacha columns. Sources: https://www.maariv.co.il/culture/article-1193260 ; https://www.kipa.co.il/חדשות/מעלעל/%d7%9e%d7%a2%d7%9c%d7%a2%d7%9c-113/?amp=1
8. Datinfo (datinfo.co.il) – PWA (browser, no app store) for local religious info: synagogues + prayer times, shiurim (topic, rabbi, level), gemachim, mikvaot, community events board. Content entered by religious councils, municipalities, gabbaim, rabbis – each with own admin panel. Source: https://datinfo.co.il/
9. Union of Synagogues app (2022) – prayer times, aliyot, donations, shiurim, events; works on kosher phones; piloted in 20 synagogues. Source: https://www.inn.co.il/news/568016
10. Glatiul (glatiul.co.il) – Shabbat-observant travel guide + events calendar (since Pesach 5784) searchable by city and artist, filtered to kosher venues; works with השדולה למען השבת and rabbinic advisers; events cluster on חול המועד, בין הזמנים. Source: https://glatiul.co.il/2024/04/17/הופעות-ואירועים-לחרדים-ודתיים/
11. Ticket sites with religious-audience categories: Tickchak LIVE (e.g. היכל שלמה), easy.co.il "מתאים לציבור הדתי". Sources: https://live.tickchak.co.il/ ; https://easy.co.il/list/Events?c2=5673
12. Kikar HaShabbat review of religious apps: news, minyan finders, zmanim, parasha calendars, "5 דקות תורה ביום", "דבר תורה". Source: https://www.kikar.co.il/503/302524

## C. Free open content sources usable from a static site (no server)
13. Hebcal APIs – free, no key: Shabbat times, zmanim, Jewish calendar (holidays, parasha), leyning. Content CC BY 4.0 (credit Hebcal); limit 90 requests / 10 s; JS library @hebcal/core works fully offline in the browser. Source: https://www.hebcal.com/home/developer-api
14. Sefaria API – no key needed; texts, links, topics, calendars ("daily learning" schedules, parasha, aliyot). 100+ tools built on Sefaria data. Sources: https://developers.sefaria.org/reference/getting-started ; https://voices.sefaria.org/sheets/737080

## D. General games that became content / event hubs
15. Fortnite "March Through Time" (TIME Studios + Epic, Aug 2021): educational MLK experience in-game; criticised as disrespectful in tone ("that guy from Fortnite"). Lesson: sacred/serious content inside a game needs its own respectful space. Sources: https://www.gameinformer.com/2021/08/26/epic-and-time-studios-launch-martin-luther-king-jr-educational-experience-in-fortnite ; https://www.cgmagonline.com/news/fortnite-mlk-tribute-criticism/
16. Fortnite concerts / cross-entertainment hub. Source: https://www.midiaresearch.com/case-studies/fortnite-a-cross-entertainment-oasis
17. Animal Crossing + Met (406,000 images) / Getty: museums built QR generators so players bring real art into their in-game museum. Lesson: content owners come to the game when there is an easy import path. Source: https://www.gamesradar.com/au/animal-crossing-new-horizons-players-can-now-import-real-artworks-using-getty-museums-generator/
18. Pokémon GO sponsored locations: McDonald's Japan 3,000 stores, 7,800 Starbucks; Niantic claims 500M visits driven, partners pay < $0.50 per daily unique visit. Lesson: a game can send players to real places and get paid for it. Source: https://techcrunch.com/?p=1497142
19. Duolingo: 37M DAU, 100M+ MAU; streaks are the #1 DAU lever, 9M users with 1-year+ streaks; weekly leagues with reset; friend streaks/quests; 75% revenue subscriptions, 9% ads. Source: https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2025/4/14/duolingo-how-the-15b-app-uses-gaming-principles-to-supercharge-dau-growth

## E. Constraints found
20. Israel Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13 in force since 14 Aug 2025: stronger enforcement, fines, compensation without proof of damage up to 10,000 NIS. Any user accounts / event submissions / contact data = personal data. Source: https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/legal/74023/164240/
21. Content filters (NetFree etc.) are widely used by the haredi/religious public; unknown sites and external links/images may be blocked until approved. (Search results only, page not fetched: https://www.jdn.co.il/economy/2360537/ ). Treat as UNVERIFIED detail.
22. Project facts: game is a static GitHub Pages site, no server. Management phase already plans 3 knowledge trees (ספר המידות volumes), contracts library, world map of shipments, friends leaderboard (stage 1 local/family code, stage 2 online).
