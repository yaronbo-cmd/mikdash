## 1. Six rules of fun for this game

1. **One touch, one act you can see, one answer from the world, all within 100 ms. The number comes last and lands somewhere.** Juice is the world answering the player, and it adds to the feel only when it rides a real act ([Jonasson & Purho](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fy0aCDmgnxg); [Nijman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJdEqssNZ-U); [Hicks et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1145/3311350.3347171): embellishment lifts appeal, and competence only in some conditions; vibration lifts player experience: [Singhal & Schneider 2021](https://uwaterloo.ca/haptic-experience-lab/projects/juicy-haptic-design-vibrotactile-embellishments-can-improve)).
2. **Anticipation is half the reward, so show the moment coming.** The ring closes, the wheel speeds up, the stone's shadow shrinks onto its slot, the coin ring is almost full. Dopamine signals the cue that predicts a reward ([Schultz, Dayan & Montague 1997](https://www.gatsby.ucl.ac.uk/~dayan/papers/sdm97.html)). We build that cue from reliable, visible signs, never from chance.
3. **Rewards are fixed, known and earned. Chance is never the thrill.** Uncertainty itself drives a ramp of dopamine ([Fiorillo, Tobler & Schultz 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12649484/)). That is the gambling hook, and in teenagers loot-box spending goes with problem gambling ([Zendle, Meyer & Over 2019](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/146026)). What varies here is the player's own grade.
4. **Skill shows.** The gold window, the gold streak and a craft's level each add a new nuance, never only bigger numbers. Competence and autonomy are what pull players back ([Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski 2006](https://www.rochester.edu/news/printable.php?id=2719)). The stage's last part lets the player show mastery ([Hayashida 2012](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-secret-to-i-mario-i-level-design)).
5. **The building remembers.** Every act leaves a lasting mark, and progress toward the next goal is always visible. We love what we build with our own hands ([Norton, Mochon & Ariely 2012](https://dash.harvard.edu/handle/1/12136084)), renovation metas work by visible achievement ([GameRefinery 2022](https://mobidictum.com/case-study-why-renovation-favorite-in-casual-games/)), and effort speeds up as the goal nears ([Kivetz et al. 2006](https://business.columbia.edu/insights/chazen-global-insights/goal-gradient-hypothesis-resurrected-purchase-acceleration)).
6. **Every stage brings a new verb, a new space and one set piece, in four beats: teach, develop, twist, conclude** ([GMTK on 3D World](https://www.mcvuk.com/development/video-nintendos-level-design-secrets-in-four-steps); Fares: "something new around every corner", [GamingBolt 2021](https://gamingbolt.com/narrative-games-can-become-very-repetitive-josef-fares-promises-max-variety-for-it-takes-two)).

## 2. The crafts

**Binding for every craft (CRAFTA19 and CRAFTB19):**

**The touch.**
- Touch-down starts the kohen's act. His first frame comes within 50 ms (the contract allows 100 ms).
- No act starts without a touch. Nothing is automatic, not even the first strike.

**The impact.**
- The world answer, the "+N", the sound and the vibration all fire on the **impact frame**, never on touch-down.
- Between strokes a striking tool is held up in a **ready pose**. Touch-down is the fall, and the **impact comes at +90 ms**. (Today the swing takes 0.40 s to impact, `RHYTHM.cycle` in content.js and the swing in stages.js. That goes.)

**The ring (rhythm crafts).**
- It is **≥ 110 px** across, drawn round the work button and mirrored over the work object.
- It touches the gold band at *beat − 90 ms*, so a tap there lands the impact on the music's beat.
- Grading is on the impact: **gold if |impact − beat − 40 ms| ≤ 90 ms; good if ≤ 180 ms; otherwise ok.** The 40 ms is the touch-latency allowance (one constant).
- At L1 the gold window is ±120 ms.

**Points per scoring act.**
- **ok 10 · good 20 · gold 40.**
- A **gold streak** (from the 3rd gold in a row) pays 50 a gold and lights "קצב!".
- A miss ends the streak. It never subtracts.

**Pace.** Every craft is tuned to about **one scoring act a second**, so no craft is a farm. The treadwheel scores a *stride* (two steps), the rub scores a *tile*, the wheel scores a *turn*.

**Taps alone finish every job.**
- On a non-tap craft a tap does one unit of the act, graded at most *good*.
- The craft's own gesture is what earns gold.

**Craft level.** Level is the job's level L1–L3 (jobs.js `levelOf`). L2 and L3 change the *task*, not only the numbers.

## 3. Every stage

**The stage's four beats:**
1. **Teach:** the survey, then the lead craft at L1.
2. **Develop:** the lead craft in the courses.
3. **Twist:** the set piece, which twists the lead craft.
4. **Conclude:** the moment and the reward.

**Fixed reward at every stage:**
- Today's `REWARD[id]`.
- **★ +1,000.**
- The stage's **seal** (חותם): a collection of 15 seals in the book, each one the lead tool and the stage's measure (המחשה).

**Leads.** No two neighbours in `STAGE_IDS` lead with the same craft, and no two neighbours in the play order (wall → gates → nashim → sorag → nikanor → lishka) do either.

**Tags.** (W20) marks work for the next wave. Everything else exists or is in this wave's rows.

## 4. The hooks (HOOK19)

### 4.1 The master score `G.score` (saved, never lost)

**What scores:**

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| A scoring act: ok · good · gold | 10 · 20 · 40 |
| A gold in a gold streak (from the 3rd in a row) | 50 |
| A job's end: ★ · ★★ · ★★★ | 50 · 100 · 200 |
| The first time a craft is played at this stage | +100 ("חדש") |
| A course's end | +250 |
| A stage's end | +1,000 |
| A ספר מידות entry read (once each) | +500 |
| A quiz question answered right (first time) | +200 |
| **Welcome**: the first stone set (once) | +1,000 |

The welcome gift is endowed progress: the coin ring starts at 10 % ([Kivetz et al.](https://business.columbia.edu/insights/chazen-global-insights/goal-gradient-hypothesis-resurrected-purchase-acceleration)).

**The first coin in about 10 minutes:**
- Good play averages about 25 a scoring act.
- At about 1 act a second, about 60 % of the time, that is ≈ 900 a minute.
- Job bonuses add ≈ 150 a minute.
- So the first coin comes at **minute 8–10**, counting the welcome gift. An average child gets there by about minute 13.
- The test asserts 8–14 minutes in a simulated session.

**How it shows:**
- A capsule in the top bar: **"★ 12,340"**.
- Beside it the coin icon, with **a ring that fills toward the next 10,000**.
- At ≥ 90 % the ring glows softly and the digits turn gold: the anticipation (rule 2).
- One "+N" flight; both the craft's xp and the score rise (contract 5).

**The דינר זהב (המחשה):**
- **Not money:** it is never spent, never lost, and buys nothing.
- **Timing:** when the score crosses a multiple of 10,000, the celebration waits for the next natural pause (a job's end, ≤ 30 s). Meanwhile the ring stays full and glowing.
- **The celebration (3–4 s, a tap skips it):**
  - The coin spins out of the capsule to the centre and lands with a metallic two-partial ring.
  - The stamp reads "דינר זהב!". The coin's face shows **the stage where it was earned**.
  - The music lifts 2 semitones for the next 8 bars.
  - The coin flies to the **תיבת הדינרים**.
- **Kept** in `G.dinars = [{ n, stage, t }]`. Shown as "◉ 3" in the capsule. A tap opens the box: a page in the book tab ("האוצר שלי").
- **What it is for (all fixed and known in advance):**
  - **A collection:** the faces, one per stage, shown with what is had and what is still to come ([GameRefinery 2021](https://gamerefinery.com/attracting-and-retaining-players-with-collection-systems)).
  - **A title ladder** on the kohen's name: 1 פועל · 3 אומן · 6 אומן בכיר · 10 ראש אומנים · 15 בונה הבית (המחשה).
  - **From the ulam:** "תלה בגפן". The coin becomes a golden leaf on the vine over the Heichal's door and stays there; the coin is still listed, marked "נתלה" (מדות ג, ח, verbatim in content.js).

### 4.2 The tabs that call

The tabs: צוותים · מלכים · ספר מידות · משימות · הפרויקט.

**When a nudge may show:**
- Only at a **natural pause**: a job's result or a course's end.
- Never during an act, a moment or a card, and never in the first 2 minutes.

**What a nudge is:**
- One line from the mentor in the bubble.
- A **gold arrow** from the line to the tab for 1.5 s.
- **One pulse** of the tab.
- The badge number stays until the tab is opened.

**Caps:**
- One nudge at a time, **at most one every 4 minutes of play**.
- Each tab at most once in 15 minutes.
- Two ignored nudges for a tab silence it until the next stage. The badge stays.

**The lines (המחשה):**

| Tab | When | The line |
|---|---|---|
| ספר מידות | After the survey's number | "המידה נכנסה לספר מידות. קרא ותקבל ★500" |
| ספר מידות | At a course's end, when an entry for this stage is unread | "בספר מידות מחכה לך משנה על {השלב}" |
| צוותים | A crew idle for over 60 s | "צוות אחד מחכה לך. שלח אותו לחצוב" |
| מלכים | A ship in port | "הספינה בנמל. בוא לפרוק" |
| משימות | A task done and not collected | "משימה הושלמה! בוא לקחת את הענב" |
| הפרויקט | A new stage open or a find added | "נפתח שלב חדש: {השלב}" |

**Learning pays (fixed):**
- An entry `at` the current stage, opened and scrolled to its end, then "קראתי ✓" (no timer, no minimum seconds): **★ +500 and +5 craft xp**, once per entry.
- Each stage's map card shows a **4th star: "כוכב הספר"**. It is lit when one of that stage's entries has been read.
- At the end of an entry, a single **"שאלו בבית"** line, a question for the family table: "כמה אמות היה הר הבית? בדקו אם אבא יודע". Children learn more when a parent talks it over with them ([Takeuchi & Stevens 2011](https://www.kqed.org/mindshift/18279/with-media-parents-and-kids-learn-more-together)).

### 4.3 Music cues for a craft's delight

All procedural (music.js and audio.js).

**Rules for every cue:**
- **A touch's own sound is instant and never snapped to the beat.**
- A *big* cue waits for the next downbeat, by at most half a beat.

| Event | The cue |
|---|---|
| Gold act | A bright pentatonic bell. Its pitch climbs one degree per gold in the streak (up to 5), then falls back gently. |
| Gold streak of 3 | The hi-hat or shaker layer for 8 bars. |
| Wheel or tread at the sweet speed | The shaker locks to 16ths. The wheel's creak is tuned to the key. |
| Cord snap | A whip-crack and **one beat of stop-time**, then everything back on the downbeat. |
| Stone lands, column stands, arch holds | A low tom and a pad swell on the downbeat. |
| Rub panel done | A one-bar shimmer gliss. |
| Saw: board falls | A woody clack on the beat, and the bass walks down. |
| Water arrives | A harp-like arpeggio. |
| Nikanor's steps | One rising scale degree per heave. |
| Job ★★★ | A 4-note rising motif on the downbeat. |
| Course end | Today's big (3–5 s) with a brass layer. |
| Dinar | The coin's ring and a +2-semitone lift for 8 bars. |

None of it is liturgical. Night and Shabbat change the colour, not the pulse (contract 6).

## 5. What not to do, and why

- **A number without an act.** It teaches the thumb that the screen is a slot machine and kills "I did that" (rule 1).
- **Anything random:** chests, spins, crits, lucky multipliers, "mystery" rewards. These are gambling mechanics (rule 3). Our "find" is a fixed reward revealed with drama (PRINCIPLES §6).
- **Fake near-misses** ("so close!" when it was not). They are manipulative, and they train chasing.
- **Loss of any kind:** streaks that reset, points that drop, coins spent away, decay. Fear is not fun for a child, and the building must only grow.
- **Timers, energy, FOMO, a shop at the peak, real money.** These are our rules (PRINCIPLES §0, §6), and they pressure children.
- **Red, a sad sound, or a stop on a miss.** A miss still builds (PRINCIPLES §4).
- **Pop-ups during an act, or nudges more than once in 4 minutes.** They break flow, and a nagging tab gets ignored.
- **A walk between two acts at one place.** Note 16, owned by NAV19.
- **A second gesture on the same craft at L1, or a gesture without a tap fallback.** Young children must finish every job.
- **Leaderboards and comparing children.** Family play stays gentle: the seal and the "שאלו בבית" line are for talking, not ranking.
- **Holiness:** never inside the Kodesh HaKodashim (the camera stops at the opening), no Ark, no gore, no blood at the altar, no iron on the altar's stones (the wooden mallet), never a Divine Name in any text, letter or engraving.
- **Assets:** no image or audio files (procedural only). The bundle is at 3.24 of 5 MiB.

## 6. ⚑ שאלות לירון (עם ברירת מחדל)

- ⚑ **מה עושים בדינר הזהב?**
  - ברירת מחדל: לא קונים בו ולא מאבדים אותו.
  - הוא אוסף: פני השלב שבו הרווחת אותו.
  - הוא נותן תואר בסולם קבוע.
  - מהאולם תולים אותו כעלה זהב בגפן (מדות ג, ח).
- ⚑ **האם ללמוד בספר מידות כתנאי למעבר שלב?**
  - ברירת מחדל: לא חוסם.
  - "כוכב רביעי — כוכב הספר" לכל שלב, ו-★500 לכל קטע שנקרא.
- ⚑ **שמירות קיימות — ניקוד על שלבים שכבר נבנו?** ברירת מחדל: 500 לכל שלב שהושלם, פעם אחת, בחגיגה אחת ("חשבון הבונה").
- ⚑ **חלון הזהב:** ברירת מחדל: ‎±90 מ"ש (ברמה 1: ‎±120), "טוב" ‎±180 מ"ש. לילדים צעירים — להרחיב?
- ⚑ **קודש הקדשים:**
  - ברירת מחדל: מובילים בגלגל הדריכה על הגג, ומורידים את תיבות האומנים (מדות ד, ה).
  - המצלמה נעצרת בפתח ולא נכנסת.
- ⚑ **ניקנור:** ברירת מחדל: מושכים את דלתות הנחושת בחבלים במעלה ט״ו המעלות. זו המחשה, ומעלה נדלקת בכל משיכה.
- ⚑ **"שאלו בבית":** שאלה אחת למשפחה בסוף כל קטע בספר. ברירת מחדל: כן.
