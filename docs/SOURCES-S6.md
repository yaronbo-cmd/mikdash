## 1. THE KIYOR AND THE WASHING (WATER, PEOPLE, BUILD's kiyor moment, MATCH3's kiyor piece)

**1.1 Exodus 30:18–19** (he: שמות ל, יח–יט) — «וְעָשִׂיתָ כִּיּוֹר נְחֹשֶׁת וְכַנּוֹ נְחֹשֶׁת לְרׇחְצָה» … «וְרָחֲצוּ אַהֲרֹן וּבָנָיו מִמֶּנּוּ אֶת־יְדֵיהֶם וְאֶת־רַגְלֵיהֶם»
— A bronze basin on a bronze stand, for washing; the kohanim wash hands and feet from it. Safe. (Do not quote v. 20: it holds the Name.)

**1.2 Middot 3:6** (he: מדות ג, ו) — «הַכִּיּוֹר הָיָה בֵּין הָאוּלָם וְלַמִּזְבֵּחַ, וּמָשׁוּךְ כְּלַפֵּי הַדָּרוֹם»
— Its place: between the Ulam and the altar, drawn toward the south. Safe.

**1.3 Yoma 3:10** (he: יומא ג, י) — «בֶּן קָטִין עָשָׂה שְׁנֵים עָשָׂר דַּד לַכִּיּוֹר, שֶׁלֹּא הָיוּ לוֹ אֶלָּא שְׁנַיִם. וְאַף הוּא עָשָׂה מוּכְנִי לַכִּיּוֹר, שֶׁלֹּא יִהְיוּ מֵימָיו נִפְסָלִין בְּלִינָה»
— Ben Katin gave the kiyor twelve spigots (it had two) and made the mukhni, so its water would not become unfit overnight.
Safe. (Torat Emet prints «יִהְיו» without the shuruk — a known typo; write יִהְיוּ.)

**1.4 Bavli Yoma 37a** (he: יומא לז, א; unvocalized in our corpus — quote without nikud)
- «כדי שיהיו שנים עשר אחיו הכהנים העסוקין בתמיד מקדשין ידיהן ורגליהן בבת אחת» — why twelve: the twelve kohanim of the
  Tamid wash at once. **This is the scene: twelve spigots, twelve kohanim together.**
- «שחרית במילואו מקדש ידיו ורגליו מן העליון ערבית בירידתו מקדש ידיו ורגליו מן התחתון» — morning, when full, from the upper;
  evening, as it runs low, from the lower. Our reading "upper/lower spigots, two heights" = **illus** (plain reading; ⚑).
- «מאי מוכני אמר אביי גילגלא דהוה משקעא ליה» — the mukhni is a wheel that sank the kiyor.

**1.5 Rashi on the Mishnah, Yoma 37a** (he: רש״י יומא לז, א) — «גלגל לשקעו בו בבור»
— a wheel to sink it into a pit (בור) at night. **Pit depth, the drum, the chains: illus.** WATER's floor-level pit mouth fits.

**1.6 Bavli Zevachim 19b — how they wash, and the posture** (he: זבחים יט, ב)
- «כֵּיצַד מִצְוַת קִידּוּשׁ? מַנִּיחַ יָדוֹ הַיְמָנִית עַל גַּבֵּי רַגְלוֹ הַיְמָנִית, וְיָדוֹ הַשְּׂמָאלִית עַל גַּבֵּי רַגְלוֹ הַשְּׂמָאלִית, וּמְקַדֵּשׁ»
  — right hand on the right foot, left hand on the left foot, and he washes (hand and foot together under the water).
- R. Yose b. R. Yehuda (both hands one on the other, on both feet one on the other) — the answer: «הִפְלַגְתָּה, אִי אֶפְשָׁר לַעֲשׂוֹת כֵּן». Follow the Tanna Kamma.
- Why not sit? «וְשֵׁירוּת מְעוּמָּד הוּא» — service is done standing: **he stands and bends down; never seated, never kneeling.**
- «כׇּל כִּיּוֹר שֶׁאֵין בּוֹ כְּדֵי לְקַדֵּשׁ אַרְבָּעָה כֹּהֲנִים מִמֶּנּוּ – אֵין מְקַדְּשִׁין בּוֹ» — a kiyor must hold enough for four kohanim.
- **For WATER/PEOPLE:** `POSES.wash` = standing, deep bend at the hips, right palm on the right instep, left on the left;
  the spigot's stream falls on hand-on-foot and runs off to the paving. Which foot first, and in the kohen's garments — silent (illus).

**1.7 Tamid 1:4 — the first washing of the day** (he: תמיד א, ד) — «עַד שֶׁשּׁוֹמְעִין קוֹל הָעֵץ שֶׁעָשָׂה בֶן קָטִין מוּכְנִי לַכִּיּוֹר, וְהֵן אוֹמְרִים הִגִּיעַ עֵת. קִדֵּשׁ יָדָיו וְרַגְלָיו מִן הַכִּיּוֹר»
— At dawn, when the mukhni's wood is heard, "the time has come"; the first kohen washes and goes up with the silver machta. Safe.

**1.8 Tamid 3:8** (he: תמיד ג, ח) — «מִירִיחוֹ הָיוּ שׁוֹמְעִין קוֹל הָעֵץ שֶׁעָשָׂה בֶן קָטִין מוּכְנִי לַכִּיּוֹר»
— From Jericho they heard the sound of the mukhni's wood. FUN: a great wooden creak; say "כך שנינו", never "scientifically".

**1.9 Bavli Yoma 31a — Etam** (he: יומא לא, א) — «עֵין עֵיטָם גָּבוֹהַּ מִקַּרְקַע עֲזָרָה עֶשְׂרִים וְשָׁלֹשׁ אַמּוֹת»
— Abaye infers (from the Kohen Gadol's first immersion «עַל גַּבֵּי שַׁעַר הַמַּיִם») that the Etam spring stands 23 amot above
the azara floor: water could reach the Temple by gravity. How it came (an aqueduct from the south) = illus in the game.

**1.10 Pesachim 5:8** (he: פסחים ה, ח) — «שֶׁהַכֹּהֲנִים מְדִיחִים אֶת הָעֲזָרָה» — the kohanim rinsed the azara's floor (on a
Shabbat Pesach eve, against the sages' wish). Supports showing the floor rinsed with water toward the drain.

**Known vs. illustration (WATER):** twelve spigots, the mukhni (a wheel, sunk into a pit), the place (between Ulam and altar,
to the south), washing hand-on-foot standing — **source**. The spigots' layout, their two heights, the pit's depth and housing,
the wheel's drum and chains, the water's colour and flow rate — **illus**.

## 2. THE SHITIN AND THE ALTAR'S DRAINAGE (WATER; with dignity — BRIEF "No gore")

**2.1 Middot 3:2** (he: מדות ג, ב) — «וּבְקֶרֶן מַעֲרָבִית דְּרוֹמִית הָיוּ שְׁנֵי נְקָבִים, כְּמִין שְׁנֵי חֳטָמִין דַּקִּין, שֶׁהַדָּמִים הַנִּתָּנִין עַל יְסוֹד מַעֲרָבִי וְעַל יְסוֹד דְּרוֹמִי, יוֹרְדִין בָּהֶן וּמִתְעָרְבִין בָּאַמָּה, וְיוֹצְאִין לְנַחַל קִדְרוֹן»
— At the south-west corner, two thin holes "like two thin nostrils"; what is poured on the west and south yesod goes down
them, mixes into the amah (the water channel) and flows out to the Kidron. **Show:** a thin dark-red trickle into the holes, then
clear water in the channel. **Never:** pools, spray, a red channel.

**2.2 Middot 3:3** (he: מדות ג, ג) — «לְמַטָּה בָרִצְפָה בְּאוֹתָהּ הַקֶּרֶן, מָקוֹם הָיָה שָׁם אַמָּה עַל אַמָּה, וְטַבְלָא שֶׁל שַׁיִשׁ, וְטַבַּעַת הָיְתָה קְבוּעָה בָהּ, שֶׁבּוֹ יוֹרְדִין לַשִּׁית וּמְנַקִּין אוֹתוֹ»
— Below, in the floor at that corner: a place one amah by one amah, a marble slab with a fixed ring, to go down into the shit
and clean it. **Model it:** 1×1 amah white marble slab + a ring (its metal: illus), in the paving at the SW corner of the altar. Safe.

**2.3 Yoma 5:6** (he: יומא ה, ו) — «אֵלּוּ וָאֵלּוּ מִתְעָרְבִין בָּאַמָּה וְיוֹצְאִין לְנַחַל קִדְרוֹן, וְנִמְכָּרִין לַגַּנָּנִין לְזֶבֶל»
— Both remainders mix in the amah and go out to the Kidron, and are sold to gardeners as fertilizer (and are hekdesh until
then — «וּמוֹעֲלִין בָּהֶן»). Safe as text; in 3D only clear water leaves the platform.

**2.4 Which yesod** (for `pour('shirayim')`):
- Zevachim 5:1 (he: זבחים ה, א) — inner offerings: «שְׁיָרֵי הַדָּם הָיָה שׁוֹפֵךְ עַל יְסוֹד מַעֲרָבִי שֶׁל מִזְבֵּחַ הַחִיצוֹן» (west yesod; Yom Kippur too — Yoma 5:6).
- Zevachim 5:3 (he: זבחים ה, ג) — the outer chatat: «שְׁיָרֵי הַדָּם הָיָה שׁוֹפֵךְ עַל יְסוֹד דְּרוֹמִי» (south yesod).
- Tamid 4:1 (he: תמיד ד, א) — the daily Tamid: «שְׁיָרֵי הַדָּם הָיָה שׁוֹפֵךְ עַל יְסוֹד דְּרוֹמִית» (south yesod).
- So every day's pour is on the **south** yesod; Yom Kippur's inner ones on the **west**; both reach the SW corner's holes.

**2.5 Sukkah 4:9 — the libation bowls** (he: סוכה ד, ט) — «צְלוֹחִית שֶׁל זָהָב מַחֲזֶקֶת שְׁלשֶׁת לֻגִּים הָיָה מְמַלֵּא מִן הַשִּׁלּוֹחַ» …
«עָלָה בַכֶּבֶשׁ וּפָנָה לִשְׂמֹאלוֹ, שְׁנֵי סְפָלִים שֶׁל כֶּסֶף הָיוּ שָׁם» … «וּמְנֻקָּבִין כְּמִין שְׁנֵי חֳטָמִין דַּקִּין, אֶחָד מְעֻבֶּה וְאֶחָד דַּק, כְּדֵי שֶׁיְּהוּ שְׁנֵיהֶם כָּלִין בְּבַת אַחַת. מַעֲרָבִי שֶׁל מַיִם, מִזְרָחִי שֶׁל יָיִן»
— On Sukkot a golden flask of three log is filled at the Shiloach; up the ramp, to the left, two silver bowls, pierced like two
thin nostrils, one wide and one narrow so both empty together; west = water, east = wine. **`pour('wine'|'water')` flows from
the bowls on the altar top (SW) down into the shitin.** (R. Yehuda: the bowls were of plaster, blackened by the wine.)

**2.6 Me'ilah 3:3** (he: מעילה ג, ג) — «הַנְּסָכִים, בַּתְּחִלָּה מוֹעֲלִים בָּהֶן. יָצְאוּ לַשִּׁיתִין, אֵין מוֹעֲלִים בָּהֶם»
— The libations went out into the shitin. **Answer to WATER (4): yes — wine and water from the bowls run into the shitin.**

**2.7 Bavli Sukkah 49a** (he: סוכה מט, א)
- R. Yochanan: «שִׁיתִין, מִשֵּׁשֶׁת יְמֵי בְּרֵאשִׁית נִבְרְאוּ» … «שֶׁמְּחוֹלְלִין וְיוֹרְדִין עַד הַתְּהוֹם» — the shitin were created in the
  six days of Creation, hollowed down to the deep. (Aggadah: show as "אמרו חכמים", not as geology.)
- R. Elazar b. Tzadok: «לוּל קָטָן הָיָה בֵּין כֶּבֶשׁ לַמִּזְבֵּחַ בְּמַעֲרָבוֹ שֶׁל כֶּבֶשׁ, וְאַחַת לְשִׁבְעִים שָׁנָה פִּרְחֵי כְהוּנָּה יוֹרְדִין לְשָׁם, וּמְלַקְּטִין מִשָּׁם יַיִן קָרוּשׁ שֶׁדּוֹמֶה לְעִיגּוּלֵי דְבֵילָה»
  — once in seventy years young kohanim went down and gathered the wine, congealed like fig-cakes (and burned it in holiness).
  A lovely "find" fact.

**Known vs. illustration (WATER):** the SW corner, the two thin holes, the slab 1×1 amah with a ring, the west/south yesod,
the amah, the Kidron, the bowls and the shitin — **source**. The amah's route under the azara, covered or open, its grates, the
outlet in the platform wall, the stream down the Kidron slope — **illus** (⚑; default: a covered channel east to the Kidron
with a few grates showing clear water). The sources name no outlet; "the Kidron" fixes only the direction (east/south-east).

## 3. HERODIAN BUILDING (BUILD, SITE, FUN's mini-games and finds, PEOPLE's crews)

**3.1 The plan — Bavli Bava Batra 4a** (he: בבא בתרא ד, א). Bava ben Buta's advice to Herod, who feared Rome:
«שַׁדַּר שְׁלִיחָא; וְלֵיזִיל שַׁתָּא, וְלִיעַכַּב שַׁתָּא, וְלֶהְדַּר שַׁתָּא; אַדְּהָכִי וְהָכִי סָתְרַיתְּ» (…and built; v3 adds [לֵיהּ] twice, so quote only this far)
— "Send a messenger: a year to go, a year to stay, a year to return; meanwhile you will have torn down and built." A great
hook for a planning phase. **Safe:** Bava ben Buta as the wise elder who advised. **Never** the context (Herod killed the sages
and blinded him, 3b–4a) — leave it out for 10+.

**3.2 The preparations — Josephus, Ant. 15.11.2 (§389–390)**, Whiston, two sites (lexundria, biblestudytools), **בתרגום חופשי**:
- "began to build; but this not till every thing was well prepared for the work" → הוא לא התחיל לבנות לפני שהכין כל מה שצריך.
- "got ready a thousand waggons, that were to bring stones for the building" → אלף עגלות להובלת האבנים.
- "chose out ten thousand of the most skillful workmen" → עשרת אלפים בעלי מלאכה מן המיומנים ביותר.
- "bought a thousand sacerdotal garments for as many of the priests, and had some of them taught the arts of stone-cutters, and
  others of carpenters" → אלף בגדי כהונה לאלף כהנים, ומהם לימד סיתות אבן ומהם נגרות.
  **For BUILD:** the numbers are Josephus'; use them as the project's scale (1,000 carts, 10,000 workers, 1,000 kohanim-builders).

**3.3 How long — Ant. 15.11.5–6 (§420–421)**, בתרגום חופשי: the cloisters and outer courts "he built in eight years" (שמונה
שנים); "the temple itself was built by the priests in a year and six months" (הבית עצמו — בידי הכהנים, בשנה וחצי); at the
dedication "he sacrificed three hundred oxen" (§422). **The House itself only by kohanim** — BUILD/PEOPLE: the crews at the
`ulam`/`heichal`/`kodesh` stages are kohanim in work clothes (illus of dress).

**3.4 The great white stones**
- Ant. 15.11.3 (§391–392), בתרגום חופשי: "the temple was built of stones that were white and strong, and each of their length was
  twenty-five cubits, their height was eight, and their breadth about twelve" → אבנים לבנות וחזקות, 25 × 8 × 12 אמה.
- War 5.5.6 (§224), two sites: "some of them were forty-five cubits in length, five in height, and six in breadth"; from afar the
  House looked "like a mountain covered with snow; for as to those parts of it that were not gilt, they were exceeding white".
  → כהר מכוסה שלג (בתרגום חופשי; show without ״״). Josephus' numbers are large; show them as "יוספוס מספר".
- Bava Batra 4a: «מִי שֶׁלֹּא רָאָה בִּנְיַן הוֹרְדוֹס, לֹא רָאָה בִּנְיָן נָאֶה» (the edition brackets [מִיָּמָיו] — leave it out) … «בְּמַאי בַּנְיֵיהּ? אָמַר רַבָּה: בְּאַבְנֵי שִׁישָׁא וּמַרְמְרָא»
  … «אַפֵּיק שָׂפָה וְעַיֵּיל שָׂפָה, כִּי הֵיכִי דִּנְקַבֵּיל סִידָא» … «סְבַר לְמִשְׁעֲיֵיהּ בְּדַהֲבָא, אֲמַרוּ לֵיהּ רַבָּנַן: שִׁבְקֵיהּ, דְּהָכִי שַׁפִּיר טְפֵי, דְּמִיחְזֵי כִּי אִידְווֹתָא דְיַמָּא»
  — built of marble stones; one course set out and the next set in, to take the plaster; he thought to overlay it with gold,
  the sages said leave it, it looks like the waves of the sea. **SITE:** alternate courses slightly proud / recessed on the House.

**3.5 Rain only at night — two independent sources**
- Bavli Ta'anit 23a (he: תענית כג, א; unvocalized in our corpus): «וכן מצינו בימי הורדוס שהיו עוסקין בבנין בית המקדש והיו יורדין גשמים בלילה למחר נשבה הרוח ונתפזרו העבים וזרחה החמה ויצאו העם למלאכתן»
- Ant. 15.11.7 (§425), בתרגום חופשי: "it did not rain in the daytime, but that the showers fell in the nights" — so the work was
  not hindered. **BUILD/WORLD:** night rain on the site is sourced; daytime rain during the building is not.

**3.6 Building does not override Shabbat — Bavli Yevamot 6a** (he: יבמות ו, א):

## 4. THE KINGS' DEALS AND THE GOLD QUESTION (KINGS, MATCH3's fields, BUILD's resources)

**Frame for every court (BRIEF):** facts and deals from these sources; the king's words are short, dignified, marked

**4.1 `tyre` — cedar from Lebanon by sea to Jaffa — Ezra 3:7** (he: עזרא ג, ז):
«וַיִּתְּנוּ־כֶסֶף לַחֹצְבִים וְלֶחָרָשִׁים וּמַאֲכָל וּמִשְׁתֶּה וָשֶׁמֶן לַצִּדֹנִים וְלַצֹּרִים לְהָבִיא עֲצֵי אֲרָזִים מִן־הַלְּבָנוֹן אֶל־יָם יָפוֹא»
— silver to the quarriers and craftsmen; **food, drink and oil** to the Sidonians and Tyrians, to bring cedar from Lebanon to
the sea at Jaffa. **Correction for KINGS:** Tyre is paid in produce (grain/wine/oil), not silver; if the meter has no produce,
pay "silver for produce" and say so (illus). This is the Return-era building (Zerubbabel) → for Herod's time it is an analogy (⚑).
**Cedar in Herod's House is sourced:** Middot 3:8 «וּכְלוֹנָסוֹת שֶׁל אֶרֶז הָיוּ קְבוּעִין מִכָּתְלוֹ שֶׁל הֵיכָל לְכָתְלוֹ שֶׁל אוּלָם, כְּדֵי שֶׁלֹּא יִבְעָט»

**4.2 `rome` — Augustus and Cyprus copper — Josephus, Ant. 16.4.5 (§128)**, Whiston, two sites, **בתרגום חופשי**:

**4.7 Kings who gave gold for the House — Ezra 8:25–26** (he: עזרא ח, כה–כו): «הַהֵרִימוּ הַמֶּלֶךְ וְיֹעֲצָיו וְשָׂרָיו וְכׇל־יִשְׂרָאֵל הַנִּמְצָאִים» … «זָהָב מֵאָה כִכָּר»
— the (Persian) king, his counsellors and princes, and all Israel gave silver, gold and vessels for the House; a hundred

### The gold question — where the Temple's gold came from (for MATCH3's "gold mines" field)
- **Donations of the people:** Middot 3:8 «כָּל מִי שֶׁהוּא מִתְנַדֵּב עָלֶה, אוֹ גַרְגִּיר, אוֹ אֶשְׁכּוֹל, מֵבִיא וְתוֹלֶה בָהּ» — whoever
  vowed a golden leaf, a grape or a cluster hung it on the golden vine. Josephus, Ant. 14.7.2 (§110), two sites: the Temple was
  rich because "all the Jews throughout the habitable earth… nay, even those of Asia and Europe, sent their contributions to it".
- **Gifts of kings and converts:** Ezra 8:25–26; Yoma 3:10 (Monbaz, Helena).
- **The treasury:** Ant. 14.7.1 (§105–109), two sites: Crassus later carried off the Temple's money and "a beam of solid beaten
  gold, of the weight of three hundred minae" — evidence of the gold held (a sad episode; not for the game's text).
- **Gold mines:** no source tells of gold mines in the Land of Israel or of mined gold bought for the Temple. The mines of that age
  were far away: Diodorus 3.12.1–2 (after Agatharchides; LacusCurtius, one pass) — "At the extremity of Egypt and in the
  contiguous territory of both Arabia and Ethiopia", worked by "Captives of war" and prisoners — harsh, and not for a kids' game.
- **⚑ MATCH3 field label (default):** keep the owner's field, labelled **`מכרה הזהב — המחשה`**, with a one-line card: "הזהב של
  המקדש בא בעיקר מתרומות ומנדבות מלכים (מדות ג, ח; עזרא ח; יומא ג, י). מכרה זהב בארץ — המחשה." Alternative (fully sourced):
  rename it **`נדבות הזהב`** and make its pieces the golden vine's leaf/grape/cluster (Middot 3:8). Never show forced labour.
