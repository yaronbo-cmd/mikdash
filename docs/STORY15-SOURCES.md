## The heart's source — "הכהן לומד מקצוע" (verified 12:2x UTC against Whiston's text, lexundria.com/j_aj/15.380-15.425/wst)
- **Josephus, Antiquities 15.11.2 (Loeb §390), Whiston, verbatim:** "but got ready a thousand waggons, that were to bring stones for the building, and chose out ten thousand of the most skillful workmen, and bought a thousand sacerdotal garments for as many of the priests, and had some of them taught the arts of stone-cutters, and others of carpenters". ✓
- **Antiquities 15.11.6 (§421), verbatim:** "But the temple itself was built by the priests in a year and six months". ✓ (§390 gives the training; the reason — that the sanctuary itself was priests' work — is this sentence, not §390.)
- **The Hebrew draft line (טיוטה, בתרגום חופשי):** ״הורדוס קנה בגדי כהונה לאלף כהנים, ולימד מהם סתתים ונגרים — ואת הבית עצמו בנו הכהנים, בשנה ושישה חודשים.״ — ref: יוספוס, קדמוניות היהודים טו, יא, ב (390); טו, יא, ו (421), בתרגום חופשי. The game already carries it as STORY.priests (content.js) and bp_priests (build_process.json); wall.beams and heichal.dig quote it.
- Note for the craft's name: Josephus' Greek has λιθουργοί (stone-workers) and τέκτονες (carpenters/builders); Whiston "stone-cutters" = סתתים. The crane operator (המנופאי) is the game's craft, not Josephus' word: המחשה.
