## The contract (fixed now — post only additions on the bus)
1. **The mode.** `twin.js` exports `createTwin(ctx)`; it is ON when all hold: landscape (`innerWidth > innerHeight` and `innerWidth >= 640`), the current stage is `har`, the game is live (not the demo reel, not `__MG_AUTO`), no `?twin=0` in the URL. When ON it sets the class **`tw-on`** on the game root (`.gm`) and emits `twin` `{ on }` on the game's bus; OFF removes both. Rotating the phone flips it live.
2. **The zones (LAND17 owns the layout).** In landscape the game root carries CSS custom properties: `--tw-left-w: 45vw`, `--tw-left-top: 35vh` (the joystick zone = the left 45% of the width, from 35% of the height down) and `--tw-right-w: 230px`, `--tw-right-h: 210px` (the buttons' box at the bottom right, inside the safe area). Under `.gm.tw-on` no HUD element of LAND17's may sit in those zones; the pill/deal and the chips go to the top edge or a side menu. TWIN17 draws only inside them (its own injected CSS, prefix `tw-`).
3. **The settings (LAND17).** In index.js's settings block: `cam: 'follow' | 'director'` (default `'follow'`) and `popups: 'show' | 'book'` (default `'show'`), saved in `bh_settings` like `rhythm`; shown in the settings sheet as "מצלמה: עוקבת / במאי" and "חלונות מידע: מופיעים / רק בספר". Read through `game.settings()` (add it if missing) and the event `settings`. `'book'`: an info pop-up (a fact card, a moment's text) does not open — a small float "נוסף לספר" shows and the fact is still saved to the book. Pop-ups that ask the player something stay.
4. **The stamp and the banner (LAND17).** `ui.stamp(text, { ms = 1100 })`: a huge centred word with radial rays (like "BRAWL!"), no buttons, `pointer-events: none`. `ui.banner(text, { ms = 2200 })`: one line at the top, imperative, queued, never two at once. TWIN17 calls `ui.stamp('יוצאים לעבודה!')` when the mode turns ON at the Mount (once per session) and `ui.banner(…)` at phase changes ("הסלע מוכן!", "הנדבך הושלם!").
5. **The sound (LAND17, §5).** `music.cue('workStart')`: a half-to-one-second near-silence, then the stinger on the next beat (≤ half a beat late), then the work music enters on the beat. In twin mode at the Mount the music plays at **120–126 BPM** (a new state or the build state's tempo when `tw-on` — check with TWIN17 that the kohen's swing, timed to the music today, still matches). Touch sounds are never quantized. Effect peaks 6–10 dB above the music bed; no sad sound on a miss; the music never stops for a mistake.
6. **The input to the jobs (TWIN17).** hands.js's `runInWorld` takes `input: 'twin'`: the right button's tap → the model's tap or press at the work's point, a drag from the button → the model's drag in the work's box (the aim drawn in the world as a line or cone), the release → execute. The canvas gestures of wave 16 stay for portrait. No `.fx-mg` sheet and no result card in twin mode — the float over the work and the mentor's one line, as in wave 16.

## TWIN17 — in this order (P1 and P2 are the gate)
- **P1 walk and camera.** The floating joystick (appears where the left thumb lands in the zone; radius ≈ 60 px; a small dead zone; the knob follows; release = stop). The kohen walks along the stick's direction relative to the camera, at his walking speed, on the ground (`groundAt`; never floating, never sunk), stopped by walls, rocks, the crane and the stock (no walking through). The follow camera: one fixed bearing, 55–60° down, soft follow with a small lead toward his walk, no cuts. Pinch on the free canvas zooms out to see what is built (he may leave the screen — fine); touching the joystick brings the camera back to him. With `cam: 'director'` the camera is today's (workFrame/dealFrame). OrbitControls' one-finger rotate is off while twin is on.
- **P2 work.** Markers in the world over the Mount's work points (the deal's cards become them; the list stays reachable in the menu). Near a marker the right button wakes with the job's icon. Tap = the basic action of the place (the strike at the rock, the step at the tread wheel…); drag = the aimed action with the aim drawn in the world; release = do it. **"Power":** three pips, then a short recharge (≈ 1 s), like the ammo bar. Every job is playable by taps alone (the drag gives a better result, never required). A bad stroke still builds and pays; only a gentle "עוד קצת". The economy (`playJob` → `setShare`, `payJob`, `jobReveal`, the stars and coins) is untouched — only the input and the kohen's place change.
- **P2 feedback.** Every touch on material answers within 50 ms in four ways: particles (`dustBurst`/`sparkBurst`), a float ("+1", "מושלם!"), a small shake or haptic, a sound. A perfect stroke: a gold flash and a big "מושלם!".
- **P3 if time allows.** "יד של אומן": charges from good work, glows when ready, a tap = the craft's special (a guaranteed perfect strike). Walls between the camera and him below a height turn half-transparent (`stages.occluded`). The FTUE's two hints: "גררו כאן כדי ללכת" at the left thumb, "הקישו כדי לעבוד" at the button.
- **test/twin-touch.cjs** at 844×390 (`hasTouch`, `isMobile`, `__START_PAUSED`, real CDP touch with two fingers at once): the left drag walks him ≥ 6 units the right way and the release stops him; he stays on the ground (± 0.1) and never passes a rock; the right tap by a rock strikes (the pips empty after 3 and refill); the four responses land within 50 ms of a strike; no `.fx-mg`, no `.gm-deal.on` while working; while walking his screen point stays in the middle third; `cam: 'director'` brings back workFrame; the first action within 10 s of entering; portrait (390×844) shows no twin control.
