## The contract (fixed now — post only additions on the bus)
1. **Points accumulate (JUICE18 + HUD18).** Every number that pops in the world flies to a counter at the top and is added there; nothing pops that doesn't land somewhere.
   - The "+1" of a work act = **נקודות מלאכה** of the craft being played (the strike → הסתת, the wheel → המנופאי, the saw → הנסר, the cord → המודד, the crane → המנופאי, the key stone → החוצב…; JUICE18 names the crafts from jobs.js `trade`). `craftxp.js`: `add(craft, n) → { xp, level, up }`, `get(craft)`, saved in the game's save (one field, `G.xp = { craft: n }`), levels at 10 · 25 · 50 · 100 · 175 · 275 points (level 1 … 7). A level up: the stamp "<המקצוע> — רמה N!" (ui.stamp), the banner, the music's flourish, and "יד של אומן" fully charged.
   - Coins and materials that a job really pays fly to their own chips (`.gm-res`) — already counted by the economy; only the flight and the chip's bump are new.
   - HUD18 provides **`ui.counter(key) → DOMRect | null`** for `'xp'`, `'coins'`, and each material key of `.gm-res`, and **`ui.bump(key, delta)`** (the chip swells ≈ 1.25×, its number ticks up, ≈ 220 ms). HUD18 reserves the slot **`.gm-top .gm-xp`** (the craft's icon, its name, "רמה N", a bar) and renders it from `ui.xp({ craft, icon, name, level, xp, next })`. JUICE18 owns the flight (a pooled DOM/CSS layer, no per-frame allocation) and calls `ui.bump`/`ui.xp`.
2. **The tabs at the top (HUD18).** Under `.tw-on` (and in landscape generally) the five tabs — צוותים, מלכים, ספר מידות, היום, הפרויקט — are visible icon buttons with their badges in the top bar (≥ 44 px each, the label under or beside the icon), never inside the thumbs' zones; the menu keeps the settings. The top bar's order (RTL): the menu ☰ at the top right corner, the tabs, the pill in the middle, the counters (xp, coins, materials) at the top left — HUD18 decides the final layout and posts it with a screenshot.
3. **Juice (JUICE18).** `juice.hit(kind, { at:{x,y,z}, text, perfect, combo, craft, quality })` — called by twin.js on every touch on material instead of its inline four answers (TWIN18 swaps the call; until then twin.js's own `answer()` stays). `juice.result({ craft, stars, coins, mats, xp, at })` at a job's end (the float, the flights, the stars popping in one by one). `juice.big(kind)` for a course's end and a stage's end (the celebration of PRINCIPLES §4: 3–5 s). Every answer within 50 ms of the touch. Whatever the recordings teach (hit-stop, impact flash, number stacking, the white flash on a reward, the scale overshoot of a stamp, the shake's curve) goes here, measured.
4. **Skill (TWIN18).** The work button shows a timing ring (a closing circle or a sweeping mark) for acts that have a moment; a tap in the gold window = מושלם (★★★ and the gold juice), near it = good, off = still builds and pays, softer. The drag stays the aimed act. Taps alone must still finish every job (no failure), but ★★★ is earned. A setting already exists for the rhythm ring (`rhythm`): off = every tap counts as good.
5. **Sound names (MUSIC18).** New `sfx.play` names posted within 30 minutes; at least: `xpTick` (a short rising tick per landing point, pitch climbing with a run), `levelUp`, `perfect`, `good`, `soft` (the off-window tap), `starPop` (n=1…3), `flyIn` (a coin/material landing), `tab` (a top tab), `stamp` and `banner` (exist). Pitch and timbre vary per hit (no identical repeats). The music: layers that enter with the work's intensity (more acts per second → more layers), a flourish on a level up and on a course's end, the hush-and-stinger of `cue('workStart')` kept.
6. **Two thumbs at every stage (TWIN18).** The mode ON in landscape at every stage of the building site (not only `har`); Phase B and the games keep today's landscape behaviour. Walls and the House's parts between the camera and him turn half-transparent (`stages.occluded`), markers only where his feet can reach, he always gets out, no sheet over a running job, the edge arrow guides him. Each stage's jobs work on the button (a job that has no world version yet may still open its sheet — list them).
