## The contract (fixed now — post only additions on the bus)
1. **One act per touch, seen (CRAFTA19 → everyone).** For every craft under two thumbs: a touch-down starts the kohen's own animation of the act within 100 ms; the act's impact frame is the scoring moment (the "+N" and the juice fire there, not at touch-down); the world object answers at the same frame. No act starts without a touch (8א: the first strike is the player's). CRAFTA19 posts within 45 minutes the gesture table in hands.js — `GESTURE[kind] = { gesture: 'tap'|'rhythm'|'swipe'|'rub'|'pull'|'hold'|'trace'|'circle', where: 'pad'|'object', anim: '<hero pose>', hint }` — and the hooks a world craft implements (`begin, act(power, quality), update(state), end, box`), so CRAFTB19 builds its crafts on the same shape.
2. **The right thumb's pad.** The gesture happens in the right part of the screen (outside the stick's zone), starting on or near the work button; the button shows the gesture as a small looping hint (a tap, a swipe arrow, a rubbing circle, a pull). `where: 'object'` crafts (letters, the cord) take the gesture on the object's screen box.
3. **The ring (CRAFTA19, 8ב).** Bigger (≥ 110 px across), drawn around the work button and mirrored over the work in the world; the gold window lines up with the kohen's impact frame; a tap's grade is measured against the impact, not against the touch. Rhythm crafts keep the tempo of the work music (HOOK19's `music.beat()`).
4. **Overview and compass (NAV19).** A button "מבט על" in the right zone above the pips (≥ 56 px): a tap lifts the camera to a high overview of the whole site with every marker and his work shown; a second tap or the stick brings it back. The compass `.tw-goal` becomes a button: a tap brings him to his work point (a quick walk at triple speed with a dust trail, or a fade-and-appear if the way is long — ≤ 1.5 s either way). Both post their API on the bus.
5. **Master score (HOOK19, 6).** `G.score` (saved): every act's points add to it (a strike 10–50 by grade, a job's end a bonus); the top bar shows it (a capsule "★ 12,340"); every 10,000 a fixed reward — a gold coin ("דינר זהב", המחשה) with its own celebration and a sound; the next milestone's progress is visible. The flight of "+N" lands in the craft's xp (as now) and in the score (one flight, both numbers rise).
6. **Sound (HOOK19, 4).** The work music (124 BPM, its layers) plays under two thumbs at every stage and at any hour of the game's day; the night and Shabbat moods may change the colour, not the pulse, while working. Prove it with a test that walks the stages.
7. **The tabs wake up (HOOK19, 20).** Each tab gets a reason to be opened: a badge with a number when something new is there, a pulse once, and every few minutes of work a mentor nudge with one line and a gold arrow to the tab ("רוצה לדעת מה בדיוק אתה בונה? פתח את ספר מידות — פרק 2 נפתח"). Learning pays: a short read in ספר מידות about the current stage gives a fixed reward (points + a craft-xp bonus). Never blocking, never a timer.
