## The Tamid crafts — the shape of each (TAMIDA23, TAMIDB23)
Every step of the Tamid becomes `WORKS[id]` replacing its sheet task in tasks.js (same id, same hour, same ממונה bubble): a model on acts.js (gold on the music's beat where the act is rhythmic, counting where it is a count), a stage of props beside the אמרכל or at the real place (the altar, the ramp, the laver, the door of the ההיכל, the Ulam's steps, the דוכן), the kohanim as figures doing the act on the player's taps (the אמרכל directs; the kohen acts — the player's tap IS the kohen's act, ≤ 50 ms), the "+N" born on the piece, the mentor line (tamid.json's text + source + "טיוטה") after the end. 6–12 acts each; taps alone; a drag may be the aimed/better act, never required. The sheet games stay in the code for portrait (do not delete them); under two thumbs the works take over (`WORKS[id]` wins when registered).
- **פיס** (תמיד א,ב; יומא ב,א): the kohanim in a circle, "הצביעו": each holds up fingers (1 or 2 — a figure pose); the ממונה names a number (e.g. 7) and the count goes round from the one whose hat was taken: the player taps the next kohen in turn, counting aloud ("…5, 6, 7!") — the 7th wins the תרומת הדשן; wrong tap = "לא, הבא בתור" no penalty. Counting, never luck — say so in the mentor line.
- **תרומת הדשן** (תמיד א,ד): at the altar's ramp: climb (walk), the מחתה — scoop (tap), carry down (walk), lay at the ramp's east (tap); then the אפר to the תפוח (3 scoops). No fire shown as danger.
- **המערכה** (תמיד ב,ג–ה): lay the logs: the big array in the east (6 logs), the second for the ketoret (2), the third (1); figs/nut/oil wood from the stock (stock.take('wood')); the fire catches (flames grow).
- **הטלה** (תמיד ג,ג–ד): from the pen: the golden cup (hold = the lamb drinks), the check by torchlight (3 taps: ear, eye, foot — a clean lamb), the 93 vessels taken out (reuse close.js's tray count in reverse: 12 taps) — ⚑ default: the vessels step is shown once per day only in the morning (close.js counts them back in the evening as המחשה).
- **ברקאי** (תמיד ג,ב): the watcher on the roof — the player taps when the east brightens (a timing act on the sky's light: the horizon glows; "האיר פני כל המזרח?" → "הין"); gold within the window.
- **פתיחת השערים** (תמיד ג,7; סוכה ה,ה): the 3 blasts (3 taps on the beat — the trumpeters), the gate swings; the sound "עד יריחו" as a stamp.
- **הטבת 5 הנרות / 2 הנרות** (תמיד ג,ט; ו,א): the אמרכל at the ההיכל's door; the kohen inside at the menorah; a tap = one lamp tended (the wick trimmed, the flame steadies); 5 then 2 — the gap for the ketoret between them (teach the order in the mentor line).
- **השחיטה** (תמיד ד,א): the lamb to the ring (walk), the screen (a cloth figure) — the act is a single tap with the sound and "הכהן שחט" — **nothing red, ever**; then **זריקה** as the vessel carried to the altar's corners (2 taps at the NE and SW corners — זבחים ה,ד "שתי מתנות שהן ארבע": the vessel tilts, no liquid drawn).
- **האברים לכבש** (תמיד ד,ב–ג): 6 kohanim carry 6 parts to the ramp — 6 taps, each carrier walks; the parts as abstract pale shapes (never anatomical).
- **הקטורת** (תמיד ה,ד–ו; ו,ג): the pitum at בית אבטינס (the mortar: 3 grinds = 3 taps or a circle drag), the מחתה of coals from the altar (walk + tap), the מגרפה thrown (a tap → the clang → the Levites run), the הקטרה at the door (the kohen inside: 3 taps, the cloud rises — a soft sprite cloud, white).
