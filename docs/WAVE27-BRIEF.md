## "Done" for a work — six tests, all from the screen (binding for SLICE27; GUIDE27 owns test 1)
1. The arrow/path leads to it and the HUD line says "עכשיו: לך ל___ — ___"; a tap on the arrow walks him there.
2. The first frame shows what to do without reading: the tool in his hand, the mark on the target, the cue ring on the thing to touch.
3. ONE thumb verb per work (PLAY27 §א; a walk-then-act is allowed as "go, then do").
4. Every touch changes a thing the eye can see at phone size — the close-up is on the act (closeup.js), the hero is the actor (hero26), the change ≥ 10 % of the frame's height.
5. The end is seen before the source line (the fire, the water, the row on the ramp), then the stamp with a name.
6. A fresh player describes in one sentence what he did (the FRESH-EYES gate).
