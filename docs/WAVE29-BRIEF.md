Phase B today is a guided tour: no goal the player wants, no way to fail, no decision, no round with a result, no rival. The fix is a genre with a proven loop for ages 7–12 — **order fulfilment / time management** (Diner Dash, Papa's, Cooking Fever, Overcooked): pilgrims arrive with offerings, each has a patience bar, each needs 2–3 stations in the right order, the kohen (the player) runs between them, a round is 90 s, stars at the end, a rival's score bar runs beside yours, "עוד פעם" is one tap. **This wave builds the prototype only — no polish, no music, no new art.** Everything already built is reused as stations and props.

**Rules that CHANGED with the owner's approval (12.10 11:07):** a clock INSIDE the round (90 s) and a soft fail (a pilgrim leaves, 1 star) are allowed; a rival NPC is allowed; "היום במקדש" (the day engine) becomes a side mode ("סיור"). Unchanged: never a Divine Name anywhere (ה׳ / אלקים / א-ל) · blood per DAM25 · the player never passes the ההיכל's door · the פיס is counting · no guilt and no pressure OUTSIDE the round (no streaks, no "come back tomorrow", nothing lost) · no gambling, no real money · taps alone finish every act (a hold/drag may be the gold act) · "טיוטה" + source or "המחשה" · RTL, 44-px targets · no images · bundle < 5 MiB (now 3.95) · real touch at 844×390 · old versions untouched.

```
                                   P: { id, type:'olah'|'ken'|'mincha', icon:'🐑'|'🕊'|'🌾', patience:0..1, state:'wait'|'taken'|'atStation'|'done'|'left', route:['kiyor','rings','altar'], step, pos, el (the icon+bar over his head: LIGHT28 claim zone) }

                                   createStations({ world, kit, twin, ctx }) → { act(id, P) → Promise<{ quality:0..1, gold:boolean }>, cue(id, on), place(), clear() }   (each act ≤ 3 touches, ≤ 4 s; taps alone finish it)

src/avodah/shift/index.js    (A)   the entry: mountShift(av, opts) — the "משמרת" button on Phase B's entry card + `#shift` / `#shift=3` hash for tests; "סיור" (the old day) as the second, smaller button
```
Routes (B, in pilgrims.js): olah 🐑: kiyor → rings → altar · ken 🕊: kiyor → duchan → altar · mincha 🌾: kiyor → seals → altar. The player TAKES a pilgrim by tapping him (or the next one by tapping the gate chip); the taken pilgrim follows the hero; tapping a station (or the work button when within 3 amot) runs that station's act; a station out of order says "לא — הכיור קודם" (no penalty, 1 s); after the last station the pilgrim leaves happy (+score, a "+N" float, the combo counter). Patience runs only while waiting at the gate and while standing at a station without being acted on; at 0 he leaves (a grey puff, "הלך", the combo resets, no other penalty).
