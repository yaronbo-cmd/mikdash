# Build 128 review: what both models agree on (Claude's summary, 2026-11-16)

The stills were NOT sent (screenshot capture in the browser hung repeatedly); both judged by the text only. No sources were cited, nothing to check on Sefaria.

Scores round 1 (Gemini / ChatGPT): sea 7 / 8, winch 5 / 7.5, steps 8 / 6.5, wickets 6 / 8, closing 9 / 8.5.

## Agreed after round 2
1. Winch: a cue BEFORE each notch (rope or metal sound that sharpens as the sledge nears it, plus a visible sign in the world itself, not a drawn gauge); do NOT widen the wedge window.
2. One consistent feedback language in all stations: every state has a visible and an audible sign; never rely on vibration (navigator.vibrate does not work on iPad Safari; not tested on ירון's tablet).
3. Wickets: keep level 3 (Gemini first wanted it deleted, then withdrew); make the two rhythms clearly different per side by separate sound and visible motion; a little more learning before level 3; at level 2 tell the out-of-sync creak apart from the dry bolt.
4. Steps: a stronger reward when the template fits (highlight + a satisfying wood-on-stone sound), readable on a small tablet; the light line of a wrong fit is too small.
5. Harbour: show and sound when the door swings toward the quay, before the player errs.
6. Sea silence: 5 seconds is too long for a child; 2 to 3 seconds with the rope still swinging. This moment comes from the approved spec, so it is ירון's call, not a tuning.
7. Gate closing: make the stiff hinge audibly and visibly different so it does not feel unfair (ChatGPT; Gemini scored the closing 9 and did not object).

## Not agreed
- Mouse for two-thumb moments: they swapped sides in round 2 (Gemini now: one prop in the world; ChatGPT now: mouse plus one keyboard key). Needs a hands-on test.

## Dropped by both
- Widening the wedge window, deleting wickets level 3, shortening the sea.
- ChatGPT's round 1 score 6.5 for the steps rested on misreading that all 15 steps are played; after the clarification it withdrew the "bottleneck" claim.

## One more idea (Gemini ranked it 4 of 5, small change)
- A small constraint per step that changes the thinking: an odd gap, a problem stone, a different laying order.
