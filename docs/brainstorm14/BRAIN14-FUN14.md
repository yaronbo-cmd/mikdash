### 4.1 The first 3 seconds
- **The card shows the gesture:** a looping hand, so she picks between gestures she sees.
- **The first touch is a move.** frame.js swallows it today ("the tap that closes the how-to is not a move"). The hand plays over the live scene instead.

- **The goal shows from second 0:** the gold line, the gap, the dull plates.

### 4.2 The course loop
1. A stage opens with **B6 החוט הצבוע** (5 s: pull, snap) in place of the survey's taps, then the crane card alone for the first stone.
2. Each course: **"מה נעשה עכשיו?"**, two cards and one ↻. On a tap he walks to the spot (2–3 s) while the sheet rises.
3. The job, its stars, and coins flying into the purse (≤ 12 sprites, a rising clink).
4. The pips fill (●●○). After the last job the crane sets the course's last stones and the level or plumb stamps it "ישר!" (~7 s). Then the course beat.

### 4.3 The deck

  - never the job just played;
