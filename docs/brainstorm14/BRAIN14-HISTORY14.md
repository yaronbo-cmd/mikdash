## 4. The historian's answer

**The premise is the history.**
- Ant. 15.11.2: Herod bought priestly garments for a thousand priests, and had some taught as masons and others as carpenters.
- 15.11.6: the priests built the House itself in a year and six months.
- So his two trades are **stone and wood**, and each stage's deck should offer one of each.

**What the hands did:** quarry, haul (a thousand wagons), dress, lift, set dry, carpentry, gilding, whitewash, bronze, paving and columns (War 5.5.2), and inscriptions.
- quarry.js and dress.js already have the stone half.
- Wood, gold, plaster, bronze, paving and columns are missing. The new jobs belong there.
